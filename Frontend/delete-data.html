Delete Pix2Vid data without deleting your account

You can permanently delete an individual Pix2Vid project and its associated data while keeping your Pix2Vid account active.

Delete a project and video in Pix2Vid

  1. Sign in to your Pix2Vid account.
  2. Open My Videos.
  3. Find the video/project you want to remove.
  4. Select Delete.
  5. Review the deletion warning and confirm deletion.

What is deleted

Deleting a video/project removes that Pix2Vid project, its uploaded project content, and its associated generated video from Pix2Vid. Your Pix2Vid account remains active.

Deleting a video/project does not cancel your Pix2Vid subscription, refund credits, or delete your Pix2Vid account.

Delete your entire account

To permanently delete your Pix2Vid account and associated account data, see Delete your Pix2Vid account.

For more information about how Pix2Vid handles personal information, see our Privacy Policy.
